## My First Post

Welcome to my personal blog.

I’ll write about building software, books I’m reading, and ideas worth keeping. The site is built with Astro Cactus, authored in Markdown, and published automatically with GitHub Actions.

The system is ready. Now the content gets to be the main character.

## Why Astro

Astro produces lightweight static pages by default while retaining a component-based development experience. For a content-focused personal blog, it strikes an excellent balance between performance, writing experience, and maintainability.
